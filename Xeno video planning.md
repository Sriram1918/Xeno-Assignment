## Delivery rules (read once, internalize, then forget)

**Your setup:** Screen-recording + voice the whole time, with your **phone propped right next to the
laptop screen** as a teleprompter (the "Say" lines below live on your phone). Two rules that make this
not look like reading:
- **Prop the phone as high and as close to the webcam/lens as possible** so your eyes barely move off
  camera. Big font, scroll slowly.
- **Read a full sentence silently, then look up/at the screen action and say it.** Glance down → speak
  → glance down. Never read mid-word with your eyes pinned to the phone — that's the tell. The half-
  beat pause while you glance reads as *thinking*, which is exactly the vibe you want.
- On the **demo beats**, you're mostly narrating what's happening on screen anyway, so your eyes are on
  the laptop, not the phone — those are the easy ones. Save the phone for the **talking beats**.

**The one mindset:** You are showing a clever thing to *one* friend who is smart but busy. Not "presenting." Not "pitching." Showing.

- **Pace:** Slightly slower than feels natural. When nervous, everyone speeds up. Force a half-beat pause after each sentence. Pauses read as confidence, not gaps.
- **Chill = breath + baseline smile.** Inhale before each take. Hold a soft, relaxed half-smile as your *resting* face. Brow unfurrowed. That alone reads as "this guy's got it handled."
- **Don't memorize word-for-word.** Memorize the *one point* of each beat (written at the top). Say it in your own words. A slightly loose natural line beats a perfect robotic one.
- **Eyes on the lens**, not your screen preview, during talking beats.
- **Hands in frame, calm.** Gesture on emphasis, rest between. No pen-clicking, no face-touching.
- **Energy curve:** Start warm and grounded → build → peak at beat 7 (the proof) → land calm and certain at beat 12. Don't start at 100%; you'll have nowhere to go.

**Pre-shoot checklist (do not skip):**
- [ ] **Reset the demo** so data is pristine (the lapsed segment is full again). Rehearse the exact run 3+ times — known-good.
- [ ] Pre-pick the **one goal** and the **one opportunity card** you'll click — don't improvise on camera.
- [ ] Screen font size bumped — code and dashboard readable at 1080p.
- [ ] Notifications OFF (Do Not Disturb, close Slack/mail/phone).
- [ ] Mic test done; quiet room; light in front of your face.
- [ ] The recovered-revenue number on screen is clean and believable.
- [ ] Gemini quota fine (only `plan`/`report` cost calls — everything else is free, so re-runs are safe).

## Editing notes
- Trim dead air between beats but keep your natural half-beat pauses *inside* beats — they're the confidence.
- One-line on-screen caption per section change (**Product / Demo / Proof / Insights / Architecture / Code**) so a tired reviewer always knows where they are.
- Keep total under ~6:25. If over, tighten beat 9 or 11 first — **never** beat 7.
- The demo beats (3–8) are one continuous real run — record them in sequence on a fresh reset so the numbers stay consistent across beats (the audience size in beat 4 must match the funnel in beat 6 and the proof in beat 7).
- Watch the whole thing once at 1x with sound OFF — your face alone should read calm and confident. Any tense-looking beat on mute → re-shoot.
